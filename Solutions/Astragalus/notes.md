# Definitions
A process:
A set of interrelated or interacting activities that use inputs to deliver an intended result.
The intended result may be an output, produce, or service.
Two or more interrelated and interacting processes in series can also be referred to as a process.

A method:
A general approach to performing a process.
Two or more interrelated and interacting methods in series can also be referred to as a method

Protocol:
Designated parameters to ensure two independent executions of a method deliver the same result given the same inputs

Procedure:
A specified way to carry out an activity or a process

System: A set of interrelated or interacting elements

Strategy: plan to achieve a long-term or overal objective

Objective: result to be achieved.
Note: can apply at different levels (such as process)

Output: result of a process

verification: confirmation, through the provision of objective evidence, that specified requirements have been fulfilled
