# Whitepaper Policy

## Definitions

whitepaper
: a document that advocates a specific position or solution. A whitepaper should be grounded in research findings, but shall not itself meet the Frascati definition of research. (Commonly, it does not present findings from systematic invesigation.) It's contribution is the position it argues and the reasoning connecting evidence to recommendation.

Because this definition is intentionally broad, each whitepaper must state it's scope and purpose. Each whtiepaper must also disclose any interests the authors hold in the position it advocates.

Note: the term "whitepaper" has wide usage in the crypto space, and has come to mean many things. We do not attempt to clarify this, and leave the definition intentionally broad. Accordingly, we describe comparatively fewer requirements on this document type than on nresearch papers. Across alld document types, however, one principla remains constant: we make claims as strong as, and no stronger than, the evidence.
